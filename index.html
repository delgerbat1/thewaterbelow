Газрын төрөл. T нь харьцангуй ус дамжуулах чадвар (transmissivity). Дюпюи, Тиемийн тогтвортой төлөвийн шийдэлд хотгорын гүн T-д урвуу пропорциональ байдаг тул T-г бууруулахад хотгор гүнзгийрнэ. Гурван утга нь жишээ харьцаа; тодорхой цэгт аль нь тохирохыг өрмийн цооногийн бичлэг, насосны туршилтаар л тогтооно.Ground type. T is a relative transmissivity. In the steady-state Dupuit and Thiem solutions the dip scales inversely with T, so lower T digs a deeper dip. The three values are illustrative ratios; which one fits a real spot can only come from borehole logs and pumping tests.

Худгийн зай. Худгийг голд ойртуулахад «толин» худаг ойртож, хотгор гол руу илүү хурдан арилна; голын эргийн налуу эгц болж, илүү их голын ус татагдана.Well distance. Moving the wells toward the river brings their mirror images closer, so the dip fades faster toward the river, the slope at the bank steepens and more river water is drawn in.

Газарт татагдсан голын ус I = max(0, −q); гол руу хүрэхгүй болсон гүний ус C = q₀ − max(0, q). Зурвас I : C харьцааг үгээр харуулна; тоо нь загварын жишээ утга тул хувиар бичээгүй. The slope 40 units from the bank is compared with pumping (q) and without it (q₀). The bar shows I : C in words, not percentages, because the split is a property of this drawing, not of the Tuul. In steady state, I + C is the river’s loss of flow [S16] [S17]. Only the city-side bank is counted.

Ус газар доор хэрхэн орж, хэрхэн гардаг вэ?How does water get under the ground — and out again?

Бороо, цас, голын ус хайрга, элсний завсраар нэвчиж ордог; худаг, гол түүнийг буцааж гаргадаг. Газар доор аварга нуур биш, хөрсний жижиг завсар бүрийг дүүргэсэн ус байдаг.Rain, snowmelt and river water seep in through the gaps in sand and gravel; wells and the river draw it back out. Underground there is no giant lake, only water filling countless small spaces in the sediment.

  1. Нөхөн сэлбэлтRecharge

    Хур тунадас, цасны хайлалт, гол болон хөрсний нэвчилтээр ус газрын доорх хурдсанд нэвтэрч болно. Хэмжээ нь улирал, газар бүрт ялгаатай.Rain, snowmelt, rivers, and infiltration can replenish permeable sediments. Recharge varies with season and location.

  2. Гол–уст үеийн солилцооRiver–aquifer exchange

    Туул гол болон ойр орчмын гүний ус харилцан ус солилцож болно. Аль чиглэлд урсах нь гидравлик түвшний зөрүүгээс шалтгаална.The Tuul River and adjacent groundwater may exchange water in either direction depending on hydraulic head and conditions. [S08]

  3. Худгийн олборлолтGroundwater pumping

    Худаг ус сорох үед орчим дахь усны түвшин хотойж болно. Хотгорын хэмжээ нь олборлолт, уст үеийн шинж чанараас хамаарна.Pumping a well can lower the nearby water table, creating a cone of depression. Its shape depends on pumping and aquifer properties. [S12]

  4. Хамгаалалт ба дахин ашиглалтProtection and reuse

    Бохирдлоос хамгаалах, шугамын алдагдлыг бууруулах, техникийн хэрэгцээнд цэвэрлэсэн усыг дахин ашиглах нь цэнгэг усны дарамтыг бууруулна.Protecting water quality, fixing leaks, and using appropriately treated wastewater for industrial needs can reduce freshwater pressure.

Өвлийн мөс ус дууссан гэсэн үг биш. Гол үргэлж уст үеийг тэжээдэг, эсвэл уст үе голыг үргэлж тэтгэдэг гэж үзэж болохгүй. Урсгалын чиглэл, хэмжээг бодит газар дээр гидрогеологийн мониторинг, хэмжилтээр тогтооно.Winter ice does not mean the water is gone. A river does not always recharge the aquifer, nor does the aquifer always feed the river. Actual direction and magnitude require site-specific hydrogeological monitoring.

How the water system works

Six scientific ideas behind the cross-section.

01 · Aquifer

Groundwater is usually held in pores and fractures of soil, gravel, and rock—not one vast underground cave. Alluvial sediments along the Tuul valley can store groundwater. [S12]

02 · Water balance

Change in storage equals water entering minus water leaving. Relevant terms include infiltration, river leakage, lateral groundwater flow, pumping, and discharge.

03 · River–aquifer exchange

Rivers can recharge an aquifer, and groundwater can feed the river. Isotope and water-chemistry studies document this connection in the Tuul basin. [S08]

04 · Cone of depression

Pumping can lower hydraulic head around a well. The drawdown depends on pumping rate and duration, aquifer properties, boundaries, and river connections. [S12]

05 · Cold-region recharge

Snowmelt, frozen ground, and thawing influence the timing of recharge. River ice alone is not evidence that groundwater has been exhausted. [S14]

06 · Quality and contamination

Nitrate and poorly managed sanitation can reduce the suitability of available water. Sampling results must be interpreted within their time and geographic limits. [S09] [S10]

ΔS = Rrain + Rriver + Qin − P − Qout

This is a conceptual water balance. River exchange may reverse direction under different hydraulic conditions. No coefficients have been estimated from measurements for this report.

Executive findings

More supply capacity does not mean all water risks have disappeared.

Central finding

Ulaanbaatar's water security depends on five connected dimensions: availability, quality, equitable access, ecosystem health, and governance. The main communication mistake is reducing all five to a single claim that the city is “running out of water.”

  1. Real progress occurred. Two major facilities—a new water purification plant and a wastewater recycling plant—were commissioned in 2026. Both are significant risk-reduction investments. [S04] [S05] [S06]
  2. Capacity is not groundwater storage. The 50 million m³/year figure describes a designed maximum throughput, not the water remaining underground, actual annual delivery, or a demonstrated sustainable abstraction rate. [S04] [S13]
  3. The river and aquifer interact. River leakage can recharge an aquifer, while groundwater can sustain river flow. Direction and magnitude depend on seasons, hydraulic gradients, and pumping. [S08] [S14]
  4. Quality is part of security. A Khan-Uul study of 134 well samples identified nitrate exceedances. This does not establish contamination in every well or in River Garden tap water. [S10]
  5. Fair access matters. OECD cites an earlier estimate of approximately 17 L/person/day among ger-district kiosk users; higher-consuming households need conservation, while underserved residents need improved safe access. [S02] [S03]
Recommended conclusion

Do not state as fact that Ulaanbaatar's water will disappear by 2040. Say instead: “New infrastructure expanded potential supply and reuse capacity. Actual outcomes still require measurement; withdrawals, water quality, fair access, and ecosystem limits remain essential.”

Final evaluation and decision

Real achievements and persistent risks can both be true.

Research synthesis

Ulaanbaatar's water future cannot be reduced to one number, one building, or one slider. The 2026 infrastructure investments expanded potential supply and industrial reuse. But sustainable withdrawals still need to be assessed against recharge, the Tuul ecosystem, water quality, and equitable public access using monitoring over time. [S03] [S04] [S05] [S08]

Decision: Replace “The city is doomed” with “Water security is a shared challenge that we can measure, protect, and manage fairly.”

Priority one: Keep observed data separate from imagined scenarios. Priority two: Make water quality and inequality central. Priority three: Track actual outcomes from recycling, leakage control, and basin monitoring over time.

  • Гэр хороолол: Аюулгүй, найдвартай ус түгээх үйлчилгээ, ариун цэвэр, шинжилгээний хүртээмж шаардах; боломжтой үед усны эх үүсвэрийн чанарыг шалгуулах.

Public-facing messages

Inform without alarmism. Explain responsibility without blame.

“Where does our drinking water come from?”

Ulaanbaatar depends heavily on groundwater. The Tuul River and alluvial aquifer can exchange water. [S11] [S08]

“Why worry if there is still water?”

Water security also depends on safe quality, sustainable abstraction, reliable delivery, and who can access it.

“Who should conserve?”

High-consuming households, building management, industry, and government have different responsibilities. Asking already underserved people to use less misses the point. [S02]

“What good news is there?”

New groundwater sources, advanced treatment, and industrial water recycling were commissioned in 2026. Their ongoing outcomes must be monitored. [S04] [S05]

Practical actions by audience
  • Apartment residents: Check meters, fix leaks, ask property managers for monthly water-use and irrigation-source reports.
  • Ger district residents: Advocate for safe, reliable distribution, sanitation, and accessible water testing; test private sources when feasible.
  • Industry: Conduct water audits, verify consumption, use treated reuse where standards permit, and publish outcomes.
  • Decision-makers: Enforce basin-scale permits, monitor withdrawals, control pollution, protect essential uses, and publish data. [S03] [S13]

Evaluation of policy responses

No single intervention solves every dimension of water security.

Appraisal method: Five qualitative criteria—evidence, likely water benefit, equity, feasibility, and monitoring—were considered. These are judgment-based comparisons, not invented cost-benefit measurements. Sources were screened for date, geographic transferability, independence, and whether figures are primary observations; interventions were assessed using trade-off and distributional analysis. Priority labels are not quantitative rankings.

Public data + leakage management + verified industrial reuse + equitable service + basin protection forms a more robust portfolio than any single technology. Large reservoirs or new wellfields need independent hydrogeological and environmental appraisal before expansion.

Implementation roadmap

Start with understanding, move to measurement, then demonstrate outcomes.

0–30 days · Improve the site

Attach units, dates, and “capacity / reported statement / measurement” labels to every figure; visually separate hypothetical simulation from real data; add a 30-second river–aquifer explanation.

1–3 months · Request primary data

Ask USUG, Mongolia's Water Agency, the Tuul River Basin Administration, and relevant research institutes for monitoring wells, river discharge, quality, and industrial reuse. Clarify licensing and publication permissions.

3–6 months · Test public learning

Conduct a short voluntary, privacy-conscious survey; compare before/after understanding; report sampling and representativeness limitations.

6–12 months · Launch verified indicators

If suitable time series are provided, publish measured groundwater levels, river flow, and actual reuse in a separate dashboard. If not, clearly state that observations are unavailable.

What counts as success?

Not page views alone: (1) understanding of groundwater, (2) source engagement, (3) ability to distinguish measured from simulated data, (4) partner participation, and (5) verified datasets added or updated.

Эдгээрийг олж авахаас өмнө бодит усны ирээдүйн тооцоог батлах боломжгүй.

What remains unknown

These gaps prevent a defensible numerical forecast of local groundwater.

  1. Site-level observation series: groundwater head (meters), coordinates, datum, timestamps, seasons, and well metadata near River Garden and the Tuul.
  2. Abstraction by source: daily volume, pumping schedule, permits, and actual production for individual wellfields and private sources.
  3. Recharge and river flow: river stage/discharge, precipitation, freezing and thawing, and a validated river–aquifer model.
  4. Water quality: nitrate, microbiological and other indicators with sampling date, well type, lab method, and relevant MNS limits.
  5. Plant outcomes: monthly actual drinking-water production, actual recycled industrial water, and verified displacement of freshwater against rated 50m/18m capacities.
  6. Household inequality: updated, comparable data on apartment, ger district, and kiosk water access and quality.
Uncertainty conclusion

New infrastructure does not prove that scarcity has been permanently solved; older pre-investment projections do not prove water will run out in 2040. Demand, capacity, sustainable yield, and quality need separate observations and updated analysis. [S01] [S04] [S07]
